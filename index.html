Track recruiting runs on numbers. Most athletes can't read them.

Your mark is your résumé. But figuring out which programs it fits, and getting a coach to notice, is mostly guesswork for high school athletes and their parents.

"Am I good enough?"

Athletes don't know if their PR is a D1 walk-on mark, a D2 scholarship mark, or a D3 conference-scoring mark.

Hundreds of programs

Researching rosters and conference results school by school takes weeks most families don't have.

Coaches get flooded

Generic copy-paste emails get ignored. Coaches respond to athletes who know their program and their event group.

How it works

Three steps from "I have no idea where to start" to emails in coaches' inboxes.

01

Enter your marks

Your event(s), PRs, grad year, GPA, and where you'd want to go to school. Takes about two minutes.

02

Get your matches

Our AI compares your marks to each program's roster and conference results and sorts schools into reach, target and likely.

03

Send real emails

For every school, ClearedBar drafts a personal email to the event coach, mentioning their program and your marks, ready to send.

Emails coaches actually read

Every draft is specific to the school, the event group, and your numbers. No "Dear Coach" templates.

To: Jumps Coach, D1 Program B
Subject: Class of 2028 high jumper, 6'7" PR

Coach,


My name is Jordan, and I'm a junior high jumper with a 6'7" PR from this spring's state meet. I've been following your jumps group, and seeing two of your jumpers score at conference this year is a big reason your program is at the top of my list.


I'm carrying a 3.6 GPA and would love to learn what you look for in recruits…
